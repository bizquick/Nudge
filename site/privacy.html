Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Addly is an app for sending links, music, videos, notes, and to-do lists to people you know. This policy explains what information Addly collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email support@addlyapp.com.

The short version

  • We collect what's needed to run Addly: your account details and the things you send and do in the app.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Your nudges are visible only to the people you send them to, unless you mark one Public.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

Information we collect

Account information. Your email address, the display name you choose, and your password (stored only in a scrambled, one-way form that no one can read).

What you create and send. The nudges you send and receive, including titles, descriptions, links, categories, to-do list items, and any photos, videos, or files you upload; chat messages on nudges; reactions; likes on public nudges; and the group names you set.

Your settings in the app. Things like which nudges you've checked off, archived, favorited, or prioritized; your Favorites folders; the order you arrange nudges in; the chats you've archived; and the people, groups, or nudges you've silenced.

Device information for notifications. If you allow notifications, a notification token from Apple that lets us deliver alerts to your iPhone. It identifies your device for notifications only.

Pages you choose to share. When you share something to Addly from another app (the iPhone Share menu) or from the Addly add-on for Chrome, we receive what you chose to send: the link, its title, any photo, and your note.

We do not collect your contacts, location, or browsing history, and we don't use advertising identifiers.

The Addly add-on for Chrome

  • When you click the Addly icon, the add-on reads the address, title, and preview picture of the page you're on, so it can show you what you're about to send. That stays on your computer unless you press Send.
  • Nothing is sent to Addly until you press Send (or choose "Send to Addly" from the right-click menu and then press Send). It never reads or records the other pages you visit.
  • You sign in once with your Addly account. Your password goes only to Addly's sign-in service; the add-on keeps a private send-key and a sign-in session on your computer so it can send nudges and show your inbox. Signing out of the add-on deletes them and cancels its send-key.
  • The add-on's inbox shows the nudges sent to you and lets you check them off, using the same account and rules as the app.

The add-on's use of information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: we use it only to provide Addly's features, never for advertising, and we never sell it.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in.
  • To deliver your nudges, messages, and reactions to the people you choose.
  • To send you notifications about new nudges and messages (unless you've silenced them or turned notifications off).
  • To send account emails you request, such as password-reset codes and reminders of your username.
  • To show public nudges in the Popular tab and rank them by likes.
  • To keep Addly working, fix problems, and protect against abuse.

Who can see what

  • Your display name is visible to other Addly users, so they can find you and send you nudges.
  • Nudges, messages, and reactions are visible to the sender and the people the nudge was sent to.
  • Public nudges (ones you mark Public) are visible to everyone who uses Addly, along with your display name, their likes, reactions, and messages.
  • Uploaded photos and files are stored at a web address that's hard to guess, but anyone who has that address can open it. Don't upload anything you wouldn't want shared.

Services we use

We rely on a few trusted companies to run Addly. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase stores your account and app data, handles log-in, and stores uploaded files.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications to your iPhone.
  • Resend sends account emails such as password-reset codes.
  • Microlink receives the web address of a link you add or share to a nudge, so it can fetch the page's title and preview image.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website.

We may also share information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete your account information and the content you created. Nudges you sent to other people may remain visible to them where needed to keep their conversations intact, and copies may persist in backups for a limited time before being removed.

Your choices

  • Notifications: turn them off in iPhone Settings, or silence specific people, groups, or nudges in Addly.
  • Public nudges: nudges are private unless you choose Public when sending.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: email support@addlyapp.com from your account's email address to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account.

Security

Your information is sent over encrypted connections. Our database is set up so people can only read and change what they're allowed to: their own account, and nudges they're part of or that are public. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Addly is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update this page and the date above, and let you know in the app if the change is significant.
