Addly

Because “I’ll check it out later” is a lie.

Addly is for the links, songs, videos, and ideas you want to share with the people you know: sent as a nudge they can check off when they've actually looked at it, instead of a message that gets buried.

Addly is in early testing. It's currently available to invited testers through Apple's TestFlight.
